TightBinding

TightBindingModel stores one-electron hopping blocks, $t_{ij}(R)=\langle 0i|h|Rj\rangle$, in eV. A lattice matrix has direct lattice vectors as columns in Å; orbital centres are fractional-coordinate columns. Centres retain the cell representatives associated with the hopping table, which may lie outside the home cell.

For fractional reciprocal coordinates $\kappa$, the periodic-gauge Hamiltonian is

\[h_{ij}(\kappa)=\sum_R t_{ij}(R)e^{2\pi i\kappa\cdot R}.\]

The orbital-position gauge includes the extra phase $e^{2\pi i\kappa\cdot(\tau_j-\tau_i)}$. Both gauges have the same band energies. Hermiticity requires $t(-R)=t(R)^\dagger$; the constructor checks this relation.

The Interfacing with Wannier90 tutorial connects these objects to the operator algebra and exact-diagonalization workflow. For a fragment defined by neighboring atoms, cluster_sites selects periodic site images around specified centres. Map those sites to Wannier orbitals and pass the ordered selection to finite_cluster, as shown in that tutorial.

MOADyna.TightBinding.FiniteCluster — Type
FiniteCluster

An open cluster extracted from a tight-binding model. hamiltonian is its one-electron matrix, and orbitals maps its indices to (cell, orbital) in the parent model. Columns of positions are Cartesian positions in Å, or nothing when the parent has incomplete geometry. Construct with finite_cluster.

MOADyna.TightBinding.KPath — Type
KPath(points, distance; labels=[], breaks=[])

A sampled reciprocal-space path. Columns of points are fractional k vectors; distance gives the plotting coordinates in Å⁻¹. Set points=nothing for reference bands whose files contain only path distances.

labels contains sample_index => label pairs. Each index in breaks starts a disconnected section. This constructor preserves the supplied distances, including any gap between sections; kpath generates physical arc-length coordinates with no contribution from such jumps. Arrays are copied.

MOADyna.TightBinding.TightBindingModel — Type
TightBindingModel(hoppings; lattice=nothing, centres=nothing, labels=nothing,
                 metadata=Dict(), atol=1e-10, rtol=1e-10)

An orthonormal tight-binding model with hoppings[R][i,j] = ⟨0,i|h|R,j⟩. Supply integer translation tuples R and square matrices, including both directions of every bond. Hermiticity is checked; entries are retained as given.

Columns of lattice are direct lattice vectors in Å. Columns of centres are orbital positions in fractional lattice coordinates, in the original cell representatives. Hoppings are in eV. Geometry may be omitted for fractional-k interpolation in the periodic gauge. Orbital order and spin content are explicit; the constructor does not duplicate orbitals or subtract a chemical potential.

Input arrays are copied. Treat the stored arrays as read-only.

MOADyna.TightBinding.bloch_hamiltonian — Method
bloch_hamiltonian(model, k; coordinates=:fractional, gauge=:periodic)

Evaluate sum_R exp(2πim * dot(k,R)) * hopping(model,R). Fractional k coordinates refer to reciprocal vectors including 2π; Cartesian coordinates are in Å⁻¹.

The default periodic gauge is the Wannier convention. With gauge=:orbital, each entry also has the phase exp(2πim * dot(k, centres[:,j]-centres[:,i])). This requires orbital centres and leaves the band eigenvalues unchanged.

MOADyna.TightBinding.cluster_sites — Method
cluster_sites(lattice, positions; centres, neighbors=1:size(positions,2),
              radius, atol=1e-10)

Select sites around one or more centres in a periodic structure. Columns of lattice are direct lattice vectors and columns of positions are Cartesian site positions, both in Å. Positions need not be wrapped into the home cell.

Each entry in centres is a site index in the reference cell, or an explicit (cell, site) pair. neighbors lists reference-cell site indices whose periodic images may be added. Include an image when its distance to any centre is at most radius + atol; both distances are in Å. The search includes all images within that distance, including for nonorthogonal lattices.

Return ordered (cell, site) pairs: distinct centres first in their supplied order, then the remaining images sorted by cell and site. Centres are always included, even when absent from neighbors. Shared neighbors appear once. Site indices retain their meaning in the supplied positions; for atomic positions, map the selected atoms to orbitals before calling finite_cluster.

Example

sites = cluster_sites(lattice, atoms; centres=[1, 2], neighbors=3:9, radius=2.5)

MOADyna.TightBinding.finite_cluster — Method
finite_cluster(model; cells=[(0, ...)], orbitals=1:size(model,1))
finite_cluster(model, selection)

Extract an open cluster. The keyword form selects the same orbitals in every listed cell, in cell-major order. For an arbitrary fragment, supply an ordered selection of (cell, orbital) pairs. Matrix entries are h[a,b] = hopping(model, cell_b-cell_a)[orbital_a,orbital_b].

MOADyna.TightBinding.kpath — Method
kpath(model, vertices; intervals=100, labels=nothing, breaks=[])
kpath(lattice, vertices; intervals=100, labels=nothing, breaks=[])

Interpolate straight sections through the columns of fractional vertices. intervals is a positive integer for every section, or a vector with one count per consecutive vertex pair. labels supplies one name per vertex.

An index in breaks starts a new section at that vertex, skipping the connection from its predecessor. Distances use the reciprocal metric of lattice, whose columns are direct vectors in Å. A model must have lattice geometry.

MOADyna.TightBinding.ligand_basis — Method
ligand_basis(h, correlated; atol=0.0, rtol=nothing)
ligand_basis(cluster::FiniteCluster, correlated; kwargs...)

Rotate the ligand orbitals so their first block spans the direct coupling to the ordered correlated indices. The correlated orbitals are unchanged and appear first in the new basis. All ligand orbitals, including the remaining tail, are retained, so this is a complete unitary transformation.

Returns a named tuple with hamiltonian = U' * h * U, the full U (old rows, new columns), and index ranges correlated, direct, and tail. Use the same U with rotate to transform other operators.

Singular values above max(atol, rtol*smax) define the direct block. The default rtol is the coupling-matrix dimension times machine precision. For full column rank, its columns follow the correlated orbital order through the polar factor of the coupling matrix: the direct coupling is then Hermitian positive definite. Otherwise, the direct block uses the nonzero left singular vectors. The tail has an arbitrary orthonormal basis. coupling_residual measures direct correlated-to-tail coupling; tail_coupling measures the coupling between the direct ligand block and its tail. Discarding the tail is a further approximation when either is nonzero.

MOADyna.TightBinding.read_vaspkit_bands — Method
read_vaspkit_bands(file; labels_file=..., path=nothing, energy_column=2, atol=5e-4)

Read block-form BAND.dat or PBAND_*.dat output from VASPKIT. Distances are in Å⁻¹ and energies in eV, retaining the file's energy zero (usually its Fermi level). energy_column selects one energy column for spin-resolved BAND files.

The adjacent KLABELS file supplies tick labels when present; set labels_file=nothing to omit it. Its positions are matched within atol, which allows for the usual three-decimal output. An explicit path instead supplies coordinates and labels after checking distances. K coordinates otherwise remain unknown. Original band indices and additional named columns, including orbital projections, are retained in metadata[:band_indices] and metadata[:columns].

MOADyna.TightBinding.read_wannier90 — Method
read_wannier90(seed; interpolation=:auto, lattice=nothing, centres=nothing,
               labels=nothing, atol=1e-10, rtol=1e-10)

Read seed_hr.dat or seed_tb.dat and the available .win, .wout, _centres.xyz and _wsvec.dat companions into a TightBindingModel. seed may also be the Hamiltonian filename or a directory containing one model. The hr file is preferred when both exist unless the tb filename is supplied explicitly. The combined tb format also supplies lattice vectors and orbital centres; off-site position-operator entries are not retained. Energies remain in eV with the source energy zero. Supplied lattice vectors are columns in Å; supplied centres are columns in fractional coordinates.

interpolation=:auto uses the source declarations and version. Pair-dependent Wigner–Seitz shifts are read from wsvec, or reconstructed from the lattice, mesh and final centres when that file is missing. Already-expanded files are recognized and are not shifted again. :plain explicitly selects the ordinary degeneracy-weighted Fourier sum, and :ws requests centre-dependent shifts. Bare hr files need an explicit interpolation choice because they do not record it.

Centres come from tb data, the final wout state, or an untranslated XYZ file, in that order. An XYZ file needs a companion win file establishing its wrapping convention. If translate_home_cell=true, supply wout data or explicit centres. Hamiltonians written in cut-band or transport modes can use translated orbital representatives; these modes require explicit centres for geometric operations.

All weights are normalized once into the stored hoppings. The selected route and source metadata are recorded in model.metadata; projection text is retained as input information, without assigning final orbital characters automatically. When a win atom block is present, metadata[:atoms] contains its labels and Cartesian positions (columns in Å). Labels are preserved as written, including numeric labels used by some converters; they need not be chemical symbols. This entry describes the source cell and is absent without an atom block; operations such as supercell retain it as source metadata.

MOADyna.TightBinding.read_wannier_bands — Method
read_wannier_bands(seed_or_file; path=nothing, atol=1e-7, rtol=5e-8)

Read the bands in seed_band.dat, preserving the source energy zero in eV. An optional KPath supplies coordinates and labels after a pointwise distance check. Otherwise the result contains the file's distances and section breaks, with unknown k coordinates. A third column, when present, is retained as metadata[:projection] with the same band/point axes as the energies. The distance tolerances accommodate the eight-significant-digit output format.

MOADyna.TightBinding.read_wannier_path — Method
read_wannier_path(seed; lattice=nothing, atol=1e-6)

Read the explicit seed_band.kpt and seed_band.labelinfo.dat path when both exist, keeping the printed k coordinates and uniformly interpolating the more precise labelinfo distances within each section. This route needs no lattice input. Otherwise reconstruct the path from seed.win, including proportional interval counts and disconnected sections. A k-point file, if present alone, is checked against this reconstruction.

For reconstruction, lattice vectors are columns in Å; by default they are read from unitcellcart in the win file. atol controls componentwise coordinate checks and accommodates band.kpt's six-decimal precision. The result is a KPath.

MOADyna.TightBinding.read_wien2k_bands — Method
read_wien2k_bands(file; energy_unit=nothing, insp_file=..., path=nothing,
                  distance_scale=1/Units.bohr_angstrom, atol=1e-5)

Read case.spaghetti_ene band blocks: three source k coordinates, accumulated distance and energy. Distances are converted from bohr⁻¹ to Å⁻¹ by default; distance_scale can specify a different conversion for externally modified files.

The adjacent case.insp determines the energy unit: switch 1 means Ry on the internal scale, switch 2 means eV relative to the supplied Fermi energy. Without that file, specify energy_unit=:Ry or :eV. Energies are converted to eV with their original zero; no Fermi energy is subtracted again.

The returned path contains distances only unless a compatible path is supplied. The first three columns are preserved in metadata[:source_kpoints], without assuming they are fractional coordinates of a Wannier cell. Original band indices are retained in metadata[:band_indices].

MOADyna.TightBinding.supercell — Method
supercell(model, S; representatives=nothing, atol=1e-10, rtol=1e-10)

Reindex a periodic model into the integral supercell with lattice model.lattice*S. Every hopping is retained. Optional representatives must contain exactly abs(det(S)) distinct primitive cells modulo S.

The result is another TightBindingModel. Its metadata[:orbital_map] gives the parent (cell, orbital) for every new orbital. The zero-translation hopping is the open-cluster Hamiltonian; nonzero translations connect different clusters. Their sum at Γ is generally different from the open-cluster Hamiltonian. atol and rtol are forwarded to the new model's Hermiticity check; supply the same precision as the parent constructor when accepting rounded input.
